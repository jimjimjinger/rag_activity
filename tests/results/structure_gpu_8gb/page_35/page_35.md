### 3. Standard and Schedule 

3.1 Standard > Scoring Structure & Evaluation Rules 

Scoring Structure & Evaluation Rules 

Monitoring criteria and points vary by page type. The scoring structure shows how each monitoring result contributes to the total score.



## 1 Page Type-Based Scoring 

Select the page type to view its monitoring categories and scoring structure.Diferent criteria apply to CAT/PLP, PDP, Insight,and Industries/Support pages.



## Score Calculation 

Criteria with assigned points contribute to the total score, The dashboard calculates the score based on whether each criterion meets the defined condition.



## Monitoring-Only Items 

Items listed under Only Monitoring are tracked for status and issue detection but are excluded from the total score.
