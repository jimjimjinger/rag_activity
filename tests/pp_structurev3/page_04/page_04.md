### 1. About dashboard 

### 1.1 Purpose of dashboard 

Purpose 

## 1 Data Collection & Integrated Monitoring 

The dashboard uses product specification data from PIM together with crawling data from B2B LG.com pages to support integrated content quality monitoring.



## 2 AX Agent-Assisted Quality Improvement 

The AX Agent provides automated monitoring reports, in-depth analysis, and improvement guidance based on monitoring data. Jira and email integration supports issue follow-up and progress tracking.



## 3 Productivity Through Task Automation 

The dashboard automates crawling-based data collection, quality monitoring, score calculation, and reporting, reducing repetitive manual work.

## Improved Content Quality & Customer Satisfaction 

Standardized monitoring criteria improve product information accuracy and content completeness. Improved site quality supports higher customer satisfaction.
