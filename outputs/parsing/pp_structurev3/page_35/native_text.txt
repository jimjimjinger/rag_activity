3. Standard and Schedule
3.1 Standard > Scoring Structure & Evaluation Rules
Scoring Structure & Evaluation Rules
Monitoring criteria and points vary by page type. The scoring structure shows how each monitoring result contributes
to the total score.
1 Page Type-Based Scoring
Select the page type to view its monitoring categories and scoring structure. Different criteria apply to CAT/PLP, PDP, Insight, and Industries/Support
pages.
2 Score Calculation
Criteria with assigned points contribute to the total score. The dashboard calculates the score based on whether each criterion meets the defined
condition.
3 Monitoring-Only Items
Items listed under Only Monitoring are tracked for status and issue detection but are excluded from the total score.
