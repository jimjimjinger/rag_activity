1. About dashboard
1.1 Purpose of dashboard
Purpose
Data Collection & Integrated Monitoring
1
The dashboard uses product specification data from PIM together with crawling data from B2B LG.com pages to support integrated content quality
monitoring.
AX Agent-Assisted Quality Improvement
2
The AX Agent provides automated monitoring reports, in-depth analysis, and improvement guidance based on monitoring data. Jira and email
integration supports issue follow-up and progress tracking.
Productivity Through Task Automation
3
The dashboard automates crawling-based data collection, quality monitoring, score calculation, and reporting, reducing repetitive manual work.
Improved Content Quality & Customer Satisfaction
4
Standardized monitoring criteria improve product information accuracy and content completeness. Improved site quality supports higher customer
satisfaction.
